# Third-party code
- `extension/qrcode.js`: qrcode-generator by Kazuhiko Arase, MIT license. Makes QR codes on your device.
- `extension/vendor/tesseract/`: Tesseract.js 5.1.1 and Tesseract.js-core 5.1.1 (Apache-2.0), with the English "best (integer)" language data from tessdata (Apache-2.0). Reads text from images on your device. Licences are in that folder.
