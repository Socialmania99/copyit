Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026 · Copyit 1.2.0

Copyit does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. Everything you save stays on your device.

1. Data we collect

None. The developer receives no data from your use of Copyit. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

2. Data stored on your device

The text, links, code, images, videos, tags, folders and settings you save are stored locally in your browser using Chrome's storage (chrome.storage.local). They stay on your device. They are never uploaded to the developer or to any third party. Your preferences (Light, Dark or Auto, and your chosen colour) are stored locally in the same way. Removing the extension, or using "Clear all clips", deletes them.

3. Clipboard access

Copyit reads your clipboard only when you press "Save clipboard" or paste into Copyit. It does not monitor the clipboard in the background. It writes to your clipboard only when you press Copy or use a "Copy as" option.

4. Optional link preview

When you open a YouTube link in the Preview window, Copyit shows a small player page from socialmania99.com, which loads the video from YouTube. This happens only when you press Preview on a YouTube link. Nothing else is sent.

If "Preview links on hover" is turned on, Copyit loads the page, image or video thumbnail that a link points to. This sends a normal web request to that site (for example YouTube, for a thumbnail). You can turn this off in Settings for zero network requests.

5. Scan to open

QR codes are generated on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

5a. Page tools (optional, off by default)

"Click to copy" and "Copy text from images & videos" are off until you switch them on. When you do, Chrome asks you once to let Copyit work on websites; turning both switches off gives that permission back. With a tool on, Copyit looks at a page only when you hover or click, to find the text you chose. Text you copy goes to your clipboard and, if you leave the option on, into your Copyit list on this device.

To read text in an image or video, Copyit takes a picture of the visible part of the tab and reads it with an OCR engine that is bundled inside the extension. The picture and the text are processed on your device, are not saved unless you copy the text into your list, and are never uploaded.

5c. Preview window, copy image, pop-out player

When you open a web link in the Preview window, the page is loaded in a frame inside Copyit, straight from that website, like opening it in a tab. Only if you press "Allow website previews", Copyit removes that website's "do not show in a frame" headers for Copyit's own preview frame and for no other page. Nothing is sent to the developer.

"Copy image directly" and "Copy video frame" read the picture you right-clicked and put it on your clipboard. If the website does not allow that, Copyit fetches the picture from its own address, or copies what is visible on screen. Copyit also saves that picture as an image clip in your Copyit list, on your device only, so you can find it later. You can delete it like any other clip. The picture is never uploaded. The pop-out player (picture in picture) works only inside Copyit's own preview window, for video or audio you saved in Copyit. It moves that player into a small window on your device and sends no data anywhere. Copyit does not add a pop-out button to videos or audio on websites.

6. Exports and backups

Files you export (JSON, CSV, TXT, Markdown) are saved to your computer by you. Copyit does not send them anywhere.

7. Permissions

  • storage, unlimitedStorage: save your clips locally.
  • clipboardRead, clipboardWrite: save from and copy to your clipboard when you ask.
  • contextMenus: the right-click save options.
  • activeTab, scripting: used by the right-click items (copy text from an image or video, copy image, copy video frame) on the page you chose.
  • declarativeNetRequestWithHostAccess: lets Copyit's own preview frame show websites that block framing, only after you press Allow website previews.
  • offscreen: runs the bundled text reader in a hidden extension page.
  • Optional access to all sites: requested only when you switch on a page tool. It lets Copyit show its copy button and read the text you click. Copyit does not read your browsing history.

Without a page tool switched on, Copyit has no access to website content.

8. Support links

The heart icon opens donation pages (such as PayPal or Ko-fi) in a new tab. Those sites have their own privacy policies. Copyit shares no data with them.

9. Children

Copyit does not collect data from anyone, including children.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
