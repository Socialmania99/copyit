Page tools

Optional helpers that work on the websites you browse. Both are off until you switch them on. Chrome asks once to allow Copyit on websites, and the permission is given back when you turn them off.

Pop-out player

Open a video or audio you saved in Copyit in the preview window, then press the pop-out button. It works only for media saved in Copyit and never touches the tabs you browse, so it has no switch here.

Import & export

Import filesPick one or more files: a Copyit backup (.json), .csv, text, Markdown, HTML, SVG, images or videos. Backups are merged without duplicates.
Export your clipsJSON is a full backup with images and videos. CSV, text and Markdown hold text clips only.

Danger zone

Clear all clipsDeletes saved clips with their images and videos from this device. Folders stay. You get a few seconds to undo.
